A BETTER SHOPPING EXPERIENCE

Good shoes deserve good care.

A little care can help your sneakers stay fresh for longer. Always follow the care label and the material-specific instructions for your pair.

Clean gently

Remove loose dust with a soft brush or cloth. Test any cleaner on a small hidden area first.

Let them air-dry

Dry naturally away from direct heat. Avoid putting wet sneakers straight onto a radiator or in a clothes dryer.

Store with care

Keep shoes in a cool, dry place and allow them to air out after wearing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check the price of a pair?

Prices and availability can change. Contact Alpha Sneakers with the product name or screenshot to confirm the latest details before ordering.

How do I choose my size?

Measure both feet and compare the longer foot with the size chart for the specific shoe. If you are between sizes, ask us for guidance before confirming.

Are all styles available in every size?

Not necessarily. Stock can vary by style, colour and size, so please confirm availability before placing your order.

How do I place an order?

Browse the products and use the contact or order option on the website to ask about the pair you want. Confirm the total, delivery details and payment method directly with the seller.

Can I request more product pictures?

Yes. Send the product name or screenshot and ask for current pictures and details before making a decision.
